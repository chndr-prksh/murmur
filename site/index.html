          How this was made

          Claude searches current news, picks the closest historical events and estimates a probability for each outcome. It also estimates, for each audience, how many people hear about it, how they split, and how many pass it on. Laya, a small open decision model, separately scores the tone of each source and its reading is passed to the forecasters as one signal. The audience rates drive a spread model of 1,000,000 simulated people, run 20 times in your browser. The probabilities are model judgement and the population figures are a simulation; neither is a measurement.
